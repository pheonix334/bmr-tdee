How it works

  1. We calculate your BMR. Using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (age, gender, height, weight) — this is roughly how many calories your body burns at complete rest.
  2. We factor in your activity level. BMR × an activity multiplier gives your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) — your maintenance calories.
  3. Three targets appear. Maintenance (your TDEE), Weight Loss (−500 cal/day, roughly 0.5 kg/week), and Weight Gain (+500 cal/day).
  4. Pick a macro style. Choose which target to build macros around, then a split (Balanced, High Protein, or Low Carb) to see exact grams of protein, carbs, and fat.
  5. Push it to your tracker. "Use as my goal" sends your chosen calories and protein straight to the Calorie Tracker's daily goal.

These are population-level formulas, not individualized prescriptions — actual metabolism varies by individual.
